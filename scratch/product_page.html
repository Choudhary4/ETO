7001 - Vertical Grain Douglas Fir French Door 1-Lite with Dual Clear Tempered Glass

*Doors are unfinished (picture may show finished door)

These French Douglas Fir doors are Exterior Grade and can be used for interiors as well. The engineered construction used on these doors provides for lasting stability. All of our glass is stamped with a tempered bug. Douglas Fir's light rosy color is set off by its remarkably straight and consistent grain pattern. Douglas Fir doors, manufactured from the products in the Factory and Shop grade classification, are renowned for their beauty and performance.
 
The AB7001 solid core construction provides a much quieter environment than hollow core doors. Our unique integral panel design will not shrink or split and there is no possibility of stile and rail separation.
 
Our AB7001 doors are shipped sanded and ready to begin the painting/finishing process. Don't be misled by our competitive pricing (we are able to keep our pricing down by selling a large quantity of door everyday!) These are high end doors!
