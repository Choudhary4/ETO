ETO Doors Return & Exchange Policy
Effective Date: September 1, 2026   ·   Supersedes all prior versions
We firmly believe you will be thrilled with the products you purchase from ETO Doors. That is because we go out of our way to ensure that our quality and prices are unmatched. We understand, however, that sometimes a product may not be what you expected it to be. In that unlikely event, the following terms and procedures govern the return or exchange of any product purchased from ETO Doors Corp. ("ETO Doors," "we," "us," or "our").
Please read carefully. By placing an order with ETO Doors, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by this Return & Exchange Policy, together with our Warranty, Terms and Conditions, and Shipping Policy, each of which is incorporated here by reference. This policy applies to all orders placed on or after the Effective Date above.
1.  Definitions
	•	"Product" means any door, slab, pre-hung unit, hardware, glass, molding, jamb, or other item sold by ETO Doors.
	•	"Stock Product" means a Product that ETO Doors regularly carries in inventory in the exact size, species, finish, and configuration ordered, and that has not been altered, prepped, machined, or finished to order.
	•	"Custom or Made-to-Order Product" means any Product that is manufactured, sized, cut, machined, bored, mortised, prepped, hung, finished, stained, painted, glazed, or otherwise produced or modified to a customer specification, including any Product not carried as regular stock.
	•	"Delivery Date" means the date the Product is delivered to the address on the order, made available for will-call pickup, or first tendered for delivery, whichever occurs first.
	•	"Final Sale" means the Product is not eligible for return, exchange, refund, cancellation, or credit under any circumstances.
2.  Inspection and Acceptance at Delivery
2.1  You, or your authorized representative, must inspect every Product at the time of delivery, before signing the delivery receipt or bill of lading.
2.2  Any visible damage, shortage, or discrepancy must be noted in writing on the delivery receipt or bill of lading at the time of delivery and photographed before the driver departs. A clean, unqualified signature on the delivery receipt is conclusive evidence that the shipment was received complete and in good condition.
2.3  Concealed damage, shortage, or discrepancy must be reported to ETO Doors in writing at sales@etodoors.com within forty-eight (48) hours of the Delivery Date. The report must include the order number and clear photographs of the Product, the affected area, and all packaging, including the shipping label.
2.4  Do not discard any packaging until the Product has been fully inspected and, where applicable, any claim has been resolved. Carriers routinely deny claims where original packaging is no longer available.
2.5  Failure to comply with this Section 2 constitutes acceptance of the Product as delivered and a waiver of any claim for damage, shortage, or discrepancy against ETO Doors or the carrier.
2.6  Do not install, finish, cut, or otherwise modify any Product you believe to be damaged, defective, or incorrect. Doing so constitutes acceptance of the Product. See Section 4.
3.  Return and Exchange Process
3.1  RMA required. No Product may be returned or exchanged without a valid Return Merchandise Authorization ("RMA") number issued in advance by ETO Doors. Products shipped to us without a valid RMA number will be refused or, if accepted, held for your account without any obligation on our part to issue a refund, credit, or exchange.
3.2  How to request an RMA. Email sales@etodoors.com, or call Customer Service at 888-DOOR-ETO or (213) 622-2003. Your request must include the order number, the item(s) and quantity, the reason for the return, and photographs of the Product in its original packaging.
3.3  Deadline to request. An RMA request must be received by ETO Doors within thirty (30) calendar days of the Delivery Date. Requests received after that period will not be considered.
3.4  RMA validity period. Each RMA is valid for fifteen (15) calendar days from the date it is issued. The returned Product must be received at the designated return address within that period. Product received after the RMA expires may be refused, or accepted subject to an additional restocking deduction at our discretion.
3.5  Return address. Return Product only to the address specified on the RMA. Do not return Product to a showroom, warehouse, or manufacturer address unless we instruct you to do so in writing.
3.6  Packaging. Repack the Product in its original factory box or container with all packing materials, hardware, glass inserts, instructions, and literature included. Do not place stickers, tape, or shipping labels directly on the factory box or on the Product itself. The RMA number must be written clearly on the outer shipping carton.
3.7  Shipping the return. We recommend shipping via insured common carrier with a tracking number. Return shipping charges and insurance are your responsibility. Title and risk of loss remain with you until the Product is received and accepted by ETO Doors. We are not responsible for Product that is lost, damaged, or delayed in return transit.
3.8  Inspection of returns. All returns are subject to inspection on receipt. ETO Doors will determine, in its sole and reasonable discretion, whether the returned Product satisfies the condition requirements in Section 4.
3.9  Rejected returns. A Product that fails inspection may be (a) refused on delivery, (b) returned to you at your expense, or (c) held for up to thirty (30) days pending your shipping instructions and a prepaid label, after which it may be treated as abandoned and disposed of without credit.
3.10  Processing time. Please allow two (2) to three (3) weeks after our receipt and inspection of the returned Product for your refund or exchange to be processed.
4.  Condition Requirements
A Product is eligible for return or exchange only if every one of the following is true. These requirements apply in addition to, and not in place of, the exclusions in Section 6.
	•	New, unused, uninstalled, and in original resalable condition.
	•	Not cut, trimmed, planed, bored, mortised, hinged, machined, or otherwise modified.
	•	Not painted, stained, sealed, primed, or otherwise finished.
	•	Free of writing, markings, drilling, labels, adhesive residue, and installed hardware.
	•	Complete, including all components, hardware, glass, fasteners, and literature.
	•	In the original factory packaging with all packing materials, undamaged and unaltered.
	•	Free of exposure damage. Product left outdoors, in unconditioned space, or in a high-humidity environment is not returnable.
Installed means accepted. Any Product that has been installed, or into which any cut, bore, hinge prep, finish, or other modification has been made, is deemed accepted and is Final Sale.
5.  Fees, Charges, and Refunds
5.1  Restocking fee. A restocking fee of twenty-five percent (25%) of the purchase price of the returned Product will be charged on all returns and exchanges.
5.2  Oversized and freight Product. An additional restocking deduction, up to a combined total of thirty-five percent (35%), may apply to oversized, over-length, freight-only, or special-handling Product that requires reconditioning, re-packaging, or re-palletizing.
5.3  Shipping and delivery charges. All delivery, freight, liftgate, residential, redelivery, storage, handling, fuel, and surcharge amounts are non-refundable under all circumstances.
5.4  Promotional or discounted shipping. Where ETO Doors absorbed or discounted outbound shipping as part of a promotion, the actual outbound shipping cost incurred by ETO Doors will be deducted from any refund.
5.5  Condition deductions. Refunds may be further reduced for missing components, damage, soiling, or packaging deficiencies, based on the cost to restore the Product to resalable condition.
5.6  Refund method. Refunds are issued only to the original method of payment used for the purchase. Refunds will not be issued in cash, to a third party, or to a different card or account. Where a refund to the original method is not possible, ETO Doors may issue a company check or store credit at its discretion.
5.7  Refund timing. Refunds are issued only after the returned Product is received, inspected, and approved. Bank and card-issuer posting times are beyond our control.
5.8  No cross-shipping. Exchanges are processed only after the returned Product has been received and approved. Replacement merchandise will not be shipped in advance. If you need replacement Product sooner, place a new order; the return will be refunded in accordance with this policy.
5.9  Deposits. Deposits and progress payments on Custom or Made-to-Order Product are non-refundable.
6.  Non-Returnable Product (Final Sale)
The following Product and orders are Final Sale. They may not be returned, exchanged, cancelled, refunded, or credited:
	•	Custom and made-to-order Product of every kind
	•	Pre-hung units, and any door hung in a frame or jamb
	•	Pre-finished, factory-finished, pre-stained, and pre-painted Product
	•	Garage doors and garage door components
	•	Any Product machined, bored, prepped, cut down, or sized to order
	•	Special-order Product, including any item not carried in regular stock
	•	Clearance, closeout, discontinued, floor-model, "as-is," and final-sale-marked Product
	•	Glass, glass inserts, and mirror product
	•	Custom or cut-to-length molding, jamb, casing, and stop
	•	Product shipped outside the contiguous United States
	•	Installed Product, and Product altered in any way after delivery
7.  Fit, Measurement, and Natural Product Variation
7.1  You are solely responsible for verifying all measurements, rough-opening dimensions, door handing and swing, jamb depth, fire rating, code compliance, and product suitability before placing an order. Our sales staff may offer general guidance as a courtesy; that guidance does not transfer responsibility for order accuracy to ETO Doors.
7.2  A return based on an incorrect size, handing, style, species, or configuration selected by the customer is a customer-convenience return, subject to Section 5, and is not permitted where the Product is Final Sale.
7.3  Wood is a natural material. Variation in grain, color, texture, mineral streaking, knots, and finish absorption is inherent and is not a defect. Color as represented on screens and in printed materials is approximate. Such variation is not a basis for return, refund, or claim.
7.4  Minor variation in dimension, warp, bow, or twist within published industry tolerance (WDMA, AWI, or the applicable manufacturer standard) is not a defect.
7.5  Product must be properly stored, acclimated, sealed on all six sides, and installed in accordance with the manufacturer instructions. Failure to do so voids return and warranty rights.
8.  Warranty Claims Are Separate
Claims that a Product is defective in materials or workmanship are governed exclusively by the ETO Doors Warranty, not by this Return & Exchange Policy. Where a valid warranty claim is established, our sole obligation, and your exclusive remedy, is repair, replacement, or credit of the affected Product, at our option. A warranty claim does not create a right to return non-defective Product.
9.  Order Changes, Cancellations, and Refused Deliveries
9.1  A request to change or cancel an order must be submitted in writing and is effective only when acknowledged in writing by ETO Doors.
9.2  Orders for Custom or Made-to-Order Product may not be changed or cancelled once production or procurement has begun.
9.3  A Stock Product order cancelled after processing but before shipment may be subject to a cancellation fee of up to ten percent (10%) of the order value, plus any costs already incurred.
9.4  An order cancelled after shipment is treated as a return and is subject to all terms of this policy.
9.5  If a shipment is refused without authorization, or cannot be delivered because of an inaccurate address, access limitation, or your unavailability at a scheduled delivery appointment, you are responsible for all outbound freight, return freight, redelivery, and storage charges, and the Product remains subject to the restocking fee.
9.6  Will-call orders not collected within fifteen (15) days may be subject to storage fees, and may be deemed abandoned after sixty (60) days.
10.  Payment Disputes and Chargebacks
10.1  You agree to contact ETO Doors and allow us a reasonable opportunity to resolve any concern before initiating a chargeback, payment dispute, or claim with your card issuer, bank, or financing provider.
10.2  Initiating a chargeback does not suspend this policy. The Product remains subject to the RMA, condition, timing, and fee requirements above, and no refund or credit will be issued for Product retained by you.
10.3  Where a chargeback or payment dispute is resolved in favor of ETO Doors, or where no refund is otherwise due under this policy, the amount of the order remains payable. ETO Doors may pursue recovery of that amount, together with any chargeback and collection costs, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law and by applicable payment-network rules, and may suspend or cancel any open orders or require prepayment by other means on future orders.
10.4  By placing an order, you consent to ETO Doors providing this policy, your record of acceptance of it, your order and delivery documentation, photographs, and related correspondence to the payment network, card issuer, financing provider, or carrier in response to any dispute or claim.
10.5  For trade, contractor, and open-account customers, the collection, cost-recovery, and fee provisions of the applicable ETO Doors credit application and trade terms apply in addition to this Section 10.

Legal Review Memorandum
Companion to the revised ETO Doors Return & Exchange Policy
Not legal advice. This memorandum summarizes the changes made to the policy and identifies provisions that an attorney licensed in California should review before publication. It is drafting support, not a legal opinion, and no attorney-client relationship is created by it.
A.  What Changed, and Why
Protections added
	•	Inspection and acceptance at delivery (Section 2). The single largest gap in the prior policy. Requires damage to be noted on the delivery receipt, sets a 48-hour concealed-damage window, requires photographs, and states that non-compliance waives the claim. Without this, ETO absorbs freight damage it can no longer recover from the carrier.
	•	RMA discipline (Sections 3.1, 3.3, 3.4). The prior policy required an RMA but attached no consequence to shipping without one, and no expiry to the RMA itself. Product now must be requested within 30 days of delivery and received within 15 days of RMA issuance.
	•	Definitions (Section 1). "Custom," "Stock," and "Delivery Date" were previously undefined, which is where most final-sale disputes start. The Delivery Date definition also closes the "delivered but not accepted" gap.
	•	Objective condition standard (Section 4). "Original condition" was previously undefined. It is now a checklist that includes finishing, boring, hinge prep, and exposure damage.
	•	Fee clarity (Section 5). Adds the oversized/freight tier, recovery of promotional shipping cost, condition-based deductions, non-refundable deposits, and refund-method restrictions.
	•	Fit and measurement responsibility (Section 7). Places responsibility for size, handing, and suitability on the buyer, and states that natural wood variation and within-tolerance dimensional variation are not defects. This is the most common source of "not what I expected" returns.
	•	Warranty separation (Section 8). Prevents defect claims from being used to return non-defective product, and caps the warranty remedy at repair, replacement, or credit.
	•	Cancellation and refused delivery (Section 9). The prior policy was silent on cancellations, refused shipments, and uncollected will-call orders.
	•	Chargebacks (Section 10), limitation of liability (Section 11), no-waiver, anti-abuse, governing law, and version control (Section 12) were all absent.
Corrections to the prior text
	•	"Place the original package into its original packaging" — corrected to instruct repacking the Product into its original packaging.
	•	"Pre-Hung Unites" and the duplicate pre-hung line item — corrected and de-duplicated.
	•	"original factory boxe(s)" — corrected.
	•	The stray "Pre-Hung Units are final sale" fragment that ran into the section heading — corrected.
	•	The broken bullet structure in the address block — rebuilt as a plain address block.
	•	"within 30 days of the date products are delivered" — tied to the new Delivery Date definition and split into a request deadline and a receipt deadline.

C.  Payment Processing Notes — Authorize.Net and Zoho Payments
How the two are different, and why it matters
Authorize.Net is a payment gateway, not the acquiring bank. Its Payment Gateway Merchant Services Agreement expressly disclaims all chargeback risk ("Authorize.Net shall bear no risk with respect to Customer's sale of products or services, including, without limitation, any risk associated with card fraud or chargebacks") and requires ETO to comply with payment-network and FTC standards. The commercial terms that actually govern chargebacks therefore sit in ETO's separate merchant-account agreement with its acquirer or ISO, which should be pulled and read alongside this policy.
Zoho Payments operates as a payment facilitator, which makes ETO a sub-merchant under Zoho's master agreement rather than a direct merchant. Under the Zoho Payments terms, ETO is solely liable for chargebacks, a dispute rate above 1% may result in suspension of processing, and Zoho may set reserves against ETO's funds at its discretion. Processing suspension is a materially larger risk to the business than any single disputed order.
The practical conclusion. Neither processor gives ETO a mechanism to charge a customer for filing a dispute, and the contract language will not change how a network resolves one. What decides a dispute is whether the return policy was properly disclosed at the time of sale and whether ETO can produce the evidence. The revised § 10.3 therefore stops threatening fees and § 10.4 secures consent to share the evidence package instead.
What actually wins these disputes
Under Visa's dispute management guidelines, a merchant defending Condition 13.7 (Cancelled Merchandise/Services) prevails by showing that its return and cancellation policy was properly disclosed at the time of sale and that the cardholder did not follow it. Related conditions are 13.3 (Not as Described or Defective) and 13.6 (Credit Not Processed). The disclosure requirement is specific for e-commerce: the policy must appear either in the sequence of pages before final checkout with a click-to-accept or other acknowledgment button, or on the checkout screen adjacent to the submit button. A link to a separate policy page is acceptable only where that link forms part of the acknowledgment.
Recommended checkout acceptance language
Place this immediately above the order submit button, with a required checkbox:
☐  I have read and agree to the ETO Doors Return & Exchange Policy, including the 25% restocking fee, that delivery and freight charges are non-refundable, that returns require an RMA within 30 days of delivery, and that custom, made-to-order, pre-hung, pre-finished, and garage door products are Final Sale.
Where the cart contains a Final Sale item, add a second, separately checked confirmation naming those line items:
☐  I understand that the following items in my order are FINAL SALE and cannot be returned, exchanged, or cancelled: [item list].
Store the policy version, the acceptance timestamp, and the IP address on the order record, and repeat the summary terms on the order confirmation email and the invoice. That record is the evidence package for § 10.4.
